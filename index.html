THE WORKING METHOD

Build. Stress it. Learn. Rebuild.

Professional development is the accumulated record of solving the next real problem—not pretending every first attempt worked.

  1. 01Define

    Start with the real operating problem, constraints and intended result.

  2. 02Build

    Assemble the hardware, network, workflow or working model.

  3. 03Test

    Put it under load and expose assumptions that do not survive reality.

  4. 04Diagnose

    Trace failure to the device, connection, process or architecture.

  5. 05Repair

    Fix what failed without losing the evidence of what was learned.

  6. 06Iterate

    Document the result and carry the improvement into the next build.

THE RECOVERY MISSION

This lab already has a history.
Now it needs a way home.

The Washington lab is the physical working environment behind years of hands-on research: computers and servers, electronics, local storage, embedded systems, cabling and the 24-port Ubiquiti PoE network that connected the pieces.

It was built by doing the work—assembling systems, breaking assumptions, tracing failures, repairing what did not hold up and carrying each lesson into the next build. The equipment is now in storage in Washington. Recovering it preserves that technical history and puts proven tools back to work in Tennessee.

Once returned, the lab will support continuing research and the development of On the Double. Ninja’s Ultimate Dojo is Tony’s name for that working R&D environment: the place where the hardware is inventoried, safely powered up, mapped, tested and rebuilt into service. It is the mission for the recovered lab—not a claim that a separate facility is already funded or complete.

  1. 01
    Secure and inventory

    Confirm access, document the equipment and preserve its condition.

  2. 02
    Pack and protect

    Use proper loading materials and protect sensitive electronics for transport.

  3. 03
    Move Washington → Tennessee

    Cover the direct transportation, fuel, lodging and storage-access costs.

  4. 04
    Recommission the lab

    Clean, inspect, power up, map and return the integrated systems to service.
