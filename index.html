METHOD

Practical systems work, not theater.

The process is simple enough to say and difficult enough to do well: define the problem, build the system, stress it, diagnose the failure, repair it, and document what changed.

  1. 01Define

    Start with the operating problem and the constraints that actually matter.

  2. 02Build

    Assemble the hardware, network, workflow, or working model.

  3. 03Stress

    Put the system under real conditions and expose weak assumptions.

  4. 04Diagnose

    Trace failure to the device, connection, process, or architecture.

  5. 05Improve

    Repair what failed, retain what worked, and carry the result into the next build.
